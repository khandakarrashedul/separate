Biography

I am an environmental science researcher with a BSc and an MS in Environmental Science from Jashore University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. My work centres on microplastics pollution: its fate, transformation and transport in coastal environments, agricultural soils, water, sediments, aquaculture systems and other environmental matrices.

Since February 2022 I have worked part-time as a Research Assistant in the Environmental Laboratory of the Asia Arsenic Network, Bangladesh. I also study how microplastics interact with emerging contaminants such as per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), and I am interested in adsorption-based wastewater treatment and biochar-based environmental remediation.
