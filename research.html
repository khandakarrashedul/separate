Focus Areas

Research Interests

Microplastics: Pollution, Fate and Transport

Occurrence, fate, transformation and transport of microplastics in coastal environments, agricultural soils, water, sediments and aquaculture systems.

Microplastics and PFAS Interactions

Interactions between microplastics and emerging contaminants, particularly per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS).

Pollution Monitoring and Risk Assessment

Environmental pollution monitoring and risk assessment across environmental matrices.

Adsorption-Based Wastewater Treatment

Adsorption-based approaches for treating contaminated wastewater.

Biochar-Based Remediation

Biochar-based environmental remediation of polluted water and soil.

Heavy Metals and Environmental Chemistry

Heavy metals and potentially toxic elements in environmental matrices, environmental chemistry and water quality.
